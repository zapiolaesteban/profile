# estebangz.com — Story & Copy Audit
*Senior copywriter / narrative-strategist pass on the LIVE site. Scope: narrative arc, hero clarity (within his chosen frame), voice, hype-words, skimmability, and how the streaming agent + `save_brief` lead-capture earn their place in the story. Positioning line is fixed by design — I critique execution, not the strategic choice.*

---

## TL;DR verdict (5 bullets)

- **The story is strong and, unusually, *true* — it shows rather than tells.** The page leads with a clear thesis ("design as a business + build the AI"), then a *working* agent proves the rare half live, then eleven real builds back it up. That arc is better than 95% of design-leader sites. Most of the work now is *trimming*, not rebuilding.
- **The hero subhead is the weakest sentence on the page.** "I'm Esteban — a **Creative Director** in the business of creativity and design" buries the lede behind a tautology ("business of creativity and design") and demotes him to "a Creative Director" right after a headline that earned more. It needs one confident rewrite.
- **One phrase is doing too much heavy lifting: "the medium is the proof."** It appears at least three times (talk intro, lead build, about). Said once it's a great line; said three times it reads like a tagline he fell in love with. Keep one, kill the rest.
- **The centrepiece agent is framed as a *demo*, not as *proof of the thing he's selling*.** "Don't take my word for it. Ask." is good, but nothing tells the visitor *they are currently inside the product he wants to be hired to build*. The streaming, the voice, the lead-capture — none of it is named as evidence. This is the single biggest missed copy opportunity.
- **The `save_brief` lead-capture is completely invisible in the copy.** It's a genuinely impressive feature (the agent emails him a lead + transcript), but a visitor would never know it exists, and the agent is never positioned to offer it. Right now it's a hidden Netlify form with zero narrative. Surfacing it *tastefully* turns a hiring-manager's idle chat into a captured lead AND a live demonstration of an agent doing real work.

## The story it currently tells

Read top to bottom, the page narrates cleanly:

1. **Hero** — "I lead design as a **business** — and build the AI that scales it." Eyebrow adds a live, human detail: *"Right now: in London, running a Creative-AI hackathon I built end-to-end."* Strong, present-tense, specific.
2. **The chat moment** — "Don't take my word for it. **Ask.**" The agent is given a full screen as the centrepiece. This is the structural masterstroke: the rare claim ("I build agentic AI") is *immediately* falsifiable by the reader.
3. **About** — "A creative leader who builds — and **grows builders**." Resolves the obvious fear ("is he just a lone coder?") with the leadership-and-mentorship half.
4. **Thesis** — "Design isn't art. It's a **discipline** that moves revenue." The commercial spine.
5. **Builds** — eleven things "I *personally built*," lead card = the site itself.
6. **Experience → Capabilities → Recognition → Contact.**

The arc is: *claim → live proof → leadership reassurance → commercial conviction → evidence → credentials → invitation.* That is a real narrative, not a CV dressed up. The thing it does best: **it refuses to ask for trust it hasn't earned** — it hands you the agent and says *check*.

Where the arc sags: the **middle stretch (Builds → Experience → Capabilities)** loses the first-person tension and slides toward list-mode. Eleven build cards is generous but flattening — by card 7 ("Monday — my daily EA agent") the reader is skimming tags, not reading. And **Capabilities** ("Where I make *the difference*") substantially *repeats* About + Builds in bullet form. The story peaks early (hero + agent) and then catalogues.

## Voice & copy critique (specific lines)

**What's working:**
- **"Right now: in London, running a Creative-AI hackathon I built end-to-end."** Best line on the site. Present-tense, falsifiable, human. It makes the whole page feel *live*.
- **"The team is the asset."** / **"I won't hand work off without a working prototype — then I hand the craft to people who can take it further than I can."** This is genuine, mature leadership voice — it disarms the "egotistical builder" worry without saying "I'm humble."
- **"Built with Claude Code — by me. The site is the portfolio."** Perfect colophon. Quiet, confident, on-message.

**What's working against him:**

- **Hero subhead, line 789:** *"I'm Esteban — a **Creative Director** in the business of creativity and design."* Three problems in twelve words: (a) "a Creative Director" is an indefinite-article demotion right after a headline that claimed category-of-one; (b) "in the business of creativity and design" is a near-tautology that says nothing; (c) it delays the actual differentiator (BCG, P&L, agentic AI) to the second clause. The headline writes a cheque the first sentence doesn't cash.

- **"The medium is the proof" — repeated 3×** (talk intro line 811, lead build line 927, about line 881). Once: a mic-drop. Three times: a verbal tic. Worse, in About it's padded — *"The medium is the proof: I built it to show what's possible — now I want to lead the people who build what's next."* — which crams thesis + ambition into one overstuffed clause.

- **"Most design leaders pick one. I do both." appears in hero sub AND is the spine of About.** It's a great line but it's the *whole* rhetorical move, used repeatedly. By the third "I do both" framing it's diminishing.

- **Mild hype creep.** "prototype to production, not demos" is sharp the *first* time (capabilities) but it appears in About, the lead XP card, and Capabilities — three times. "hands-on" appears ~6 times across the page. These are good words turned into filler by repetition.

- **"Where I make *the difference*"** (Capabilities header) is the one genuinely generic, LinkedIn-flavoured heading on an otherwise specific page. It promises differentiation then lists six competencies anyone could claim.

- The agent greeting **"Hi, I'm Esteban's AI."** is fine but flat — it doesn't set up *what makes talking to it worth the visitor's time* or hint it can actually *do* something (book a follow-up).

## Hero subhead / support polish (WITHIN his "design as a business + build the AI" frame)

Headline stays: **"I lead design as a *business* — and build the AI that scales it."** It's good. The job is to make the support sentence *land the proof and the scale fast*, without re-explaining the headline.

**Current:**
> "I'm Esteban — a **Creative Director** in the business of creativity and design. I run the **Global Creative AI practice at BCG Design Studios**, where craft, technology and P&L are one job: I turn design into revenue, and I build the agentic AI that scales it. Most design leaders pick one. I do both."

**Option A — proof-first, tightest:**
> "I'm Esteban. I run the **Global Creative AI practice at BCG Design Studios** — where craft, P&L and code are one job. I turn design into revenue, and I personally build the agentic AI that scales it. The proof is below: talk to the agent I built."

**Option B — scale-and-stakes:**
> "I'm Esteban. As **Global Creative Director at BCG Design Studios** I run design as a P&L — and personally ship the agentic AI behind it. A team of 16, reach into 400+, prototype to production. Don't take my word for it — the agent below is mine."

**Option C — keep his 'I do both' beat but earn it once:**
> "I'm Esteban — **Global Creative Director & Creative AI Lead at BCG Design Studios.** I run design as a business that moves revenue, *and* I write the agentic AI that scales it. Most design leaders do one. I built the proof into this page."

All three (a) lead with the real institution, (b) cut "in the business of creativity and design," (c) hand the reader straight to the agent — so the support copy *recruits the centrepiece* instead of just restating the headline.

## How the streaming agent + `save_brief` fit the story — and how to make *using it* feel like proof

This is the highest-leverage section of the audit.

**The structural insight is already right:** the agent is the centrepiece, placed second, framed as falsifiable proof. What's missing is **copy that names what's happening as evidence.** Right now a hiring manager chats, gets good answers, and thinks "nice chatbot." He should think "*wait — he built a streaming, voice-enabled, lead-capturing agent for his own site. This is the work.*"

Three copy moves:

**1. Name the medium as the product, at the moment of use.** Add a single quiet line under the composer or in the agent foot:
> *"Everything here — the streaming, the voice, the follow-up — is hand-built on a serverless function. You're not reading about the work. You're using it."*

That converts a passive feature into an *active argument*. The visitor's own click becomes the proof.

**2. Make `save_brief` visible — and frame it as the agent *doing real work*, not a contact form.** Currently the lead-capture is a hidden Netlify form (lines 745–755) with no surface copy. A visitor never learns the agent can email Esteban a brief + transcript. That's a waste of the single most *productized* feature on the site.

Give the agent a natural offer it can make mid-conversation (driven by the system prompt, surfaced in the suggestion chips):
- Add a chip: **"I might have a role for him."** — which triggers the agent to ask 2–3 light questions and then say something like:
  > *"Want me to pass this to Esteban directly? I'll send him a short brief and our conversation so he can follow up — no form to fill."*
- The genius is the *meta-layer*: the visitor watches an agent **autonomously capture a lead and hand off to a human** — which is *exactly* the "human-in-the-loop agentic AI" he describes building ("Monday"). The lead-capture isn't a contact form bolted on; it's **a live demo of the category of work he's hired to lead.** Say that, lightly, after it fires:
  > *"Done — Esteban will get this within the hour. (That hand-off you just saw? That's the kind of agent I build.)"*

**3. Close the loop in the footer CTA.** The footer says *"Building something where design and AI are one practice? Let's talk."* — but the *easiest* way to "talk" (the agent that just offered to take a brief) isn't referenced. Add: *"Or just tell the agent up top — it'll pass it on."* Now the agent is woven through the whole arc, not stranded at the top.

Net effect: the agent stops being a clever toy at the top of the page and becomes **the recurring proof of the thesis**, with the lead-capture as its mic-drop demonstration.

## 8+ concrete copy rewrites (current → suggested)

1. **Hero sub opener** — *Current:* "I'm Esteban — a **Creative Director** in the business of creativity and design." → *Suggested:* "I'm Esteban. I run the **Global Creative AI practice at BCG Design Studios** — where craft, P&L and code are one job."

2. **About lead** — *Current:* "*I build the thing, then I lead the team that scales it.*" → *Suggested (keep, but make it the only 'medium is proof' echo):* "*I build the thing, prove it works, then grow the team that scales it.*"

3. **About, line 881 tail** — *Current:* "This site and the agent you just spoke to are hand-built. The medium is the proof: I built it to show what's possible — now I want to lead the people who build what's next." → *Suggested:* "This site and the agent you just spoke to are hand-built — on a serverless function, with my own guardrails. I'd rather show you than tell you. What I want next is to lead the people who build what's after this."

4. **Talk-section heading subcopy** — *Current:* "...Ask it anything, by text or voice. I built it: the medium is the proof." → *Suggested:* "...Ask it anything, by text or voice. It streams, it speaks, it can even pass a brief to me — all hand-built. You're not reading about the work; you're using it."

5. **Agent greeting** — *Current:* "Hi, I'm Esteban's AI. Ask about his work, the agentic AI he builds, or what's next — by text or voice." → *Suggested:* "Hi — I'm the agent Esteban built. Ask about his work, the AI he ships, or what he's after next. If you've got a role in mind, I can pass it straight to him."

6. **Capabilities heading** — *Current:* "Where I make *the difference*." → *Suggested:* "What I'm actually good at." (or "The edge, in six lines.") — drop the LinkedIn cliché.

7. **Suggestion chips** — *Current:* "What has he actually built?" / "Is he technical, or does he just manage?" / "What's he looking for next?" / "Tell me about the hackathon." → *Suggested:* keep the first three; replace "Tell me about the hackathon" with **"I might have a role for him."** (the lead-capture trigger — turns a curiosity chip into a conversion path).

8. **Builds intro** — *Current:* "All my own craft — my tools, my platforms, not client deliverables. The proof that 'creative leader who codes' is literal." → *Suggested:* "My own tools and platforms — not client work. Proof that 'creative leader who codes' is literal, not a LinkedIn line."

9. **Lead build card** — *Current:* "...runs on a serverless function with my own guardrails. **The medium is the proof.**" → *Suggested:* "...streams answers, speaks in my voice, and can email me a brief when a visitor wants to talk. **You're using it right now.**"

10. **Footer CTA add-on** — *Current:* "Building something where design and AI are *one practice?* Let's talk." → *Suggested:* add a second line: "Email me below — or just tell the agent up top. It'll pass it on."

## "Productized" recommendations (make it read as a crafted product, not a CV)

- **Cut Builds from 11 to ~7, grouped.** Eleven cards reads as inventory. Lead with the 4 hero builds (this site+agent, the multi-agent Studio, the hackathon platform, the enterprise/mining shipment) and *fold* the rest (Monday, email-sender, deck-builder, screenshot-cleaner, World Clock, AI-OS) into a tighter "and the daily toolkit I run my own work on" cluster. Fewer, sharper cards = more product, less résumé.
- **Give the agent a persistent presence, not a one-screen cameo.** A small docked "Ask the agent" affordance that follows the scroll would make the whole page feel like a product with a live assistant — and keep the lead-capture reachable from anywhere.
- **Name the `save_brief` capability somewhere it's *visible as a spec*.** In the lead build card, one line — "streams, speaks, captures leads, hands off to a human" — reads as a product datasheet, which is exactly the "robust / productized" register he's after.
- **Kill the duplicate Capabilities section or merge it into About.** It currently repeats material with less specificity. Removing it tightens the arc (claim → proof → story → evidence → invite) and removes the one generic-feeling block.
- **One "medium is the proof"-class line, used once, at the climax** (the agent), then never again. Scarcity is what makes a signature line land.

---

## Summary (4 bullets)

- **The bones are excellent and rare-because-true:** thesis → live agent proof → leadership reassurance → real builds. Don't rebuild — *trim and sharpen*. The structure already beats almost every design-leadership site.
- **Fix the hero subhead and stop repeating three good lines** ("the medium is the proof," "prototype to production not demos," "I do both"). Said once each, they're sharp; said 3–6× they read as tics and dilute the very confidence they're meant to project.
- **The biggest untapped win is the agent + `save_brief`:** name the streaming/voice/lead-capture *as the product the visitor is using right now*, and make the agent offer to pass a brief to Esteban — so a hiring manager's idle chat becomes both a captured lead and a live demo of the human-in-the-loop agentic AI he wants to be hired to lead.
- **Productize by subtraction:** cut Builds 11→~7, drop or merge the generic "Capabilities" block, keep the agent present beyond one screen. Less inventory, more product.
